01 · The Kitchen

A decade of cakes, one at a time.

Every cake here started as a conversation about the venue, the palette, the guest count, and the story behind the day. The Pastry Kitchen is no longer taking orders, so this is the archive.

How every cake was made

Sketch first. Then sugar.

  1. 01

    Consult

    Every cake began with the client: the vision, the venue, the colors, the formality, and the story they wanted the cake to tell.

  2. 02

    Sketch

    Several design directions went on paper and were reviewed together. Nothing went into production until the client had signed off.

  3. 03

    Build

    Structure, flavor, decoration and transport were all planned backward from a date that couldn’t move.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Delivery was the moment of truth. Afterward came an honest review of what worked and what to do better next time.

02 · The Canvas

Color, without the buttercream.

Choosing a palette for a wedding cake and choosing one for a landscape turn out to be much the same job. The Canvas holds watercolor landscapes and studies, and a print shop is planned.

More pieces coming soon.

03 · The Code

Structure you can hold.

A five-tier cake has to stand up, and so does a 3D-printed part or a website. The Code covers 3D-printed objects, digital fabrication, and web work like the page you’re reading, which was hand-built rather than made from a template.

The workshop is always printing something.

The Story

Before I knew what a design system was, I was building one.

I’m David R. Bailey. I ran The Pastry Kitchen for more than ten years, designing and delivering custom cakes for weddings, birthdays and every kind of celebration. I trained at Sullivan University in Louisville, Kentucky, where I earned an A.S. in Baking & Pastry Arts with honors and a Silver Medal.

That work taught me that the client’s first request is never the whole picture, that you sketch before you commit, and that a deadline is a deadline. Today I work as a UX/UI designer and technologist, and Pastry Kitchen Designs is where the making continues.
